## {Espresso Martini}

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Martini glass
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1 oz Mr. Black Cold Brew Coffee Liqueur
  - 1 oz Vodka OR Cognac
  - 1 oz. freshly brewed espresso (slightly cooled down)
  - 1/4 oz rich Demerara syrup (2:1 sugar:water)
  - Oil from one swath of lemon
- **Instruction:** Add all ingredients to the shaking tin, express the oil from lemon swath and shake vigorously. Double strain into the chilled glass. express some more lemon oil on top and add the coffee beans as garnish.
- **Garnish:** 3 roasted coffee beans
- **Profile:** Bold and rich from espresso and damerara, finish with clean lemon oil flavor. foamy and silky on palate.
