## {Long Island Ice Tea}

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** pint glass
- **Ingredients:**
  - 1 oz. Vodka 
  - 1 oz Rum 
  - 1 oz Gin
  - 1 oz Tequila (Blanco or silver) 
  - 1 oz Triple Sec
  - 1 oz lemon juice 
  - 1/2 oz rich Demerara syrup (2:1)
- **Instruction:** Shake all ingredients then open pour into chilled glass.  top up with coke for color. Garnish with lemon.
- **Garnish:** Lemon wage or lemon wheel
- **Profile:** Boozy, strong, bold and complex. Refreshing.
