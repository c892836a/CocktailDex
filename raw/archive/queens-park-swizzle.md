## Queen's Park Swizzle

**Background:** TBD

- **Glassware:** Collins glass
- **Ingredients:**
  - 2 oz Aged rum
  - 0.75 oz Lime juice
  - 0.5 oz Demerara rich syrup
  - 8–10 Mint leaves
  - 6–8 dashes Angostura bitters
- **Instruction:** Slightly muddle mint leaves, mix all ingredients except bitters with crushed ice. Swizzle and top up with more ice. Add bitters.
- **Garnish:** 1 Sprig of mint
- **Profile:** Spiced, Citrusy, Complex
